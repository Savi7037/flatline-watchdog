What happens when
your bot fails.

  • One command to start

    Start your bot through the Flatline agent. It needs no exchange keys and opens no ports.

  • The reason comes with the alert

    A crash alert includes the likely cause and the bot's last log lines, with keys and tokens hidden first.

  • A switch you control

    Stop the bot from Telegram, or choose which alerts stop it. Your own clean-up command then runs.
